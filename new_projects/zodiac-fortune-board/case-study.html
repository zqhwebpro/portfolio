What It Is & Core Vision

Zodiac Fortune Board reimagines centuries-old divination instruments—the brass astrolabe, the Major Arcana tarot deck, and Norse elder runes—as an immersive, reactive web experience. Rather than pressing generic buttons, users interact using natural body language in front of their webcam. The camera becomes an arcane Scrying Glass that translates hand poses into celestial divination spells.

Inspired by mystical sacred geometry and cinematic Eldritch sanctums (reminiscent of Doctor Strange Tao mandalas), the interface features an intricate concentric astrolabe wheel with rotating degree ticks, astrological houses, planetary runes, and dynamic trine/opposition aspect line geometry.

The 3-Second Hold Lock Architecture

A key design breakthrough was preventing the compass from drifting when a user examines their reading. By requiring the clenched fist to remain held for exactly 3.0 continuous seconds, the app distinguishes between a momentary transition and a deliberate lock command. While held, an animated circular SVG meter with live countdown HUD renders over the screen. If released early (e.g. at 2.1s), the lock sequence cancels safely. Once locked, the sign is crowned with an imperial beacon wave, wheel rotation is locked against drift, and all progressive stages focus strictly on the chosen zodiac.

Astrolabe Sacred Geometry & Dynamic Aspect Chords

The central divination compass is built on rigorous geometric and astronomical principles:

    When locked, the selected zodiac node expands to scale 1.34 with a 4px imperial gold border, dual-layer glowing aura, continuous beacon pulse animation (selectedBeaconWave), and an illuminated 👑 SELECTED badge, making the chosen sign unmistakably visible across the board.

  • 12 O'Clock Zenith Needle & Rotational Snapping

    A fixed top alignment pointer decodes the sign aligned with the Zenith needle: normalizedAngle = (-rotation % 360 + 360) % 360. As the user spins the wheel, procedural astral tick audio plays each time a 30-degree notch passes the needle.
