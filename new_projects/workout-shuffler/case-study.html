Workout Shuffler: Circuit Generation Engine

An adaptive fitness randomizer and circuit trainer with muscle balance constraints, interval rest timers, and mobile-first ergonomics.

Project Overview

Athletes frequently encounter workout fatigue and routine staleness. Workout Shuffler automates personalized routine generation across key muscle categories (Push, Pull, Legs, Core, Cardio) while giving users complete control over set tracking, tutorial previews, and custom rest intervals.

Technical Highlights

Constrained Shuffling Algorithm: Generates balanced routines by preventing muscle-group over-exhaustion and staggering high-intensity compound movements across the circuit.

Drift-Free Interval Timer: Implements audio feedback and timestamp-based interval tracking that maintains accurate countdowns even when mobile browsers enter background power-saving modes.

Mobile-Optimized HUD: Built with thumb-reachable sticky bottom controls, high-contrast visual status badges, and instant exercise swapping without losing current workout progression.

What I Learned (Core Takeaways)

  • ⏱️

    Timer Drift Prevention in Browsers

    Standard setInterval calls drift significantly when device screens sleep or tabs lose focus. Calculating remaining time against absolute Date.now() deltas ensures accurate timing during intensive workouts.

  • 🏋️

    Data Modeling for Athletic Workouts

    Structuring exercise models with rich metadata (muscle focus, equipment requirements, difficulty, and form pointers) allows seamless filtering and scalable content expansion.

  • 📱

    Ergonomic Mobile-First Design

    Users in gyms interact with apps with sweaty hands and limited attention. Large tactile touch targets, clear contrast levels, and audio beeps remove the need for constant screen monitoring.
