01 // WHAT IT IS

What It Is & Purpose

Workout Mixer is a tactical, distraction-free fitness utility designed to eliminate workout decision fatigue. Rather than navigating rigid, subscription-locked workout trees, athletes can instantly spin algorithmic circuits calibrated by equipment availability, muscle group balance, and target work/rest ratios.

The UI prioritizes high legibility in challenging gym environments—featuring high-contrast visual status indicators, haptic countdowns, and tap zones large enough for sweaty hands mid-interval.

02 // ARCHITECTURE & IMPLEMENTATION

Engineering & Algorithmic Foundations

Developed with modern JavaScript, structured state machines, and responsive CSS, the application is driven by client-side algorithmic randomization with biological constraint checking:

  • Antagonist Muscle Group Pairing

    Prevents consecutive exercises targeting identical movement planes (e.g. quad fatigue or rotator cuff strain), dynamically interleaving push/pull and upper/lower movements.

  • Drift-Proof Interval Engine

    Uses epoch delta timestamps rather than naive setInterval loops, ensuring accurate timer countdowns even when mobile browser tabs are throttled or locked in pocket mode.

  • Procedural Audio Feedback

    Synthesizes audible interval warning cues via the Web Audio API, delivering crisp bell alerts across noisy gyms without loading external sound files.
