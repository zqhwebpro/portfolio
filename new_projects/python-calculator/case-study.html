Atlas: Sovereign Chess & Win Engine

An opulent Baroque chess calculus workbench combining complete piece move generation, positional evaluation heuristics, live win probability calculation, and move delta previews.

Project Overview

Atlas provides a masterclass in chess calculation. Rather than functioning as an opaque black box, it exposes the underlying mathematical heuristics of chess evaluation in real time. Players can play against tactical AI, test sandbox moves in Free Mode, and analyze how any candidate move shifts winning probability percentages.

Technical Highlights

Complete Rule & Move Generator: Implements comprehensive chess rules (sliding raycasts for queens, rooks, bishops; knight leap offsets; pawn double pushes and en passant; king castling; and check/checkmate detection).

Evaluation Heuristics & PSTs: Combines material balance weights with piece-square positional tables (favoring central control, open files for rooks, and king shelter) to compute instant centipawn valuations and win probability curves.

Live Move Delta Previews: Hovering any active piece or candidate target square calculates immediate tactical swings (+/- win %) before committing the move.

What I Learned (Core Takeaways)

  • ♟️

    Positional Evaluation vs Pure Material Counting

    Material alone does not reflect true chess advantage. Weighting center control, knight outposts, and piece mobility through Piece-Square Tables (PSTs) provides drastically superior evaluation accuracy.

  • ⚡

    Optimizing Move Generation and Raycasting

    Pruning invalid move paths early and representing the board with efficient index offsets yields sub-millisecond calculation speeds for candidate moves across all 64 squares.

  • 🏛️

    Baroque Salon Aesthetic & Visual Feedback

    Combining wood textures, gold drop shadows, turn highlights, and Staunton SVG pieces creates a visually rich and distinctive classical interface.
