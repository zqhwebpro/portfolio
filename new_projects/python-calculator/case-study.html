01 // WHAT IT IS

What It Is & Chess Calculus

Chess PyCalc is a standalone chess calculation workbench that bridges the gap between classic board play and positional game theory. Rather than acting as an inscrutable black-box chess bot, the engine transparently computes and displays the positional score delta, center control rating, king safety index, and material balance before and after every prospective move.

Players can analyze complex midgame positions, test alternative candidate lines with an interactive move delta preview, and trace the shifting probability curve over the course of an entire match.

02 // ALGORITHMIC HEURISTICS & EVALUATION

Engineering & Positional Evaluation

Engineered in Vanilla JavaScript with deterministic state validation and evaluation algorithms:

  • Full FIDE Rule Compliance

    Implements complete special-move mechanics including en passant pawn captures, king/rook castling with check transit validation, pawn promotion dialogs, and threefold repetition detection.

  • Piece-Square Table (PST) Positional Matrix

    Combines standard material point values (Pawn: 100, Knight: 320, Bishop: 330, Rook: 500, Queen: 900) with positional heatmaps rewarding central knight outpost positioning and open rook files.

  • Sigmoid Win Probability Curve

    Translates centipawn evaluation advantages into normalized human-readable win probabilities using an empirical logistic sigmoid curve: P(win) = 1 / (1 + 10^(-score / 400)).
