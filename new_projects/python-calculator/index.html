1. Standard Algebraic Notation (SAN)
K, Q, R, B, N
Piece Identifiers King (K), Queen (Q), Rook (R), Bishop (B), Knight (N). Pawns carry no prefix letter (e.g. e4, d5).
x
Capture Symbol Indicates a capture. Pawn captures include origin file: exd5; piece captures: Nxf7, Qxd4.
O-O / O-O-O
Castling Notation O-O = Kingside Castle (short); O-O-O = Queenside Castle (long). Moves King 2 squares toward Rook.
e.p.
En Passant ("In Passing") Capturing an opposing pawn that advanced 2 squares on the immediately preceding turn as if it had only moved 1.
=Q / =N
Pawn Promotion Appended when a pawn reaches the 8th rank: e8=Q, a8=N (underpromotion).
+, ++, #
Check, Double Check & Mate + = Check, ++ = Double Check (two pieces attacking simultaneously), # = Checkmate (decisive victory).

3. Master Jargon & Tactical Lexicon
Tempo (Plural: Tempi) Time & Initiative

The basic unit of time in chess, equivalent to one turn. Winning a tempo means forcing the opponent to make a defensive reaction, allowing you to advance your tactical development ahead of schedule.

Fianchetto Italian: "On the Flank"

Developing a Bishop to b2, g2, b7, or g7 behind a pawn advance (b3/g3/b6/g6), granting the Bishop sweeping long-range control across the primary central diagonal.

Pin (Absolute vs Relative) Tactical Immobilization

An attack on a piece that cannot move without exposing a more valuable piece behind it. An Absolute Pin shields the King (illegal to move). A Relative Pin shields a Queen or Rook (legal but catastrophic).

Fork & Skewer Multi-Ray Geometry

Fork: A single piece (especially a Knight or Pawn) attacks two or more enemy pieces simultaneously. Skewer: A ray attack through a high-value piece, forcing it to retreat and surrendering the piece behind it.

Zugzwang & Zwischenzug (Intermezzo) German Master Terms

Zugzwang: A position where any move worsens the player's situation; if passing were permitted, they would pass. Zwischenzug: An intermediate, unexpected counter-move (check or capture) played before an expected recapture.

Luft & King Safety Escape Corridor

German for "air/breath". Moving a pawn in front of a castled King (e.g. h3 or h6) to create a flight square, preventing back-rank checkmates.

4. Evaluation Calculus & Probability
Centipawns (cp) Valuation

The standard engine metric for positional balance. 100 cp = 1 Pawn. Piece values: Pawn = 100 cp, Knight = 320 cp, Bishop = 335 cp, Rook = 500 cp, Queen = 900 cp, King = 20,000 cp.

Logistic Win Probability Formula

Winning chances are mathematically computed in real-time via: P(Win) = 1 / (1 + 10^(-cp / 400)) An advantage of +4.00 cp (+400 cp) yields an ~90.9% winning probability for White.
