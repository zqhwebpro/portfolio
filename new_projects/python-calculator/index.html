How to Understand this App

Chess Calculus & Move Tutor Guide

1. Centipawns & Win Probability

The engine scores positions in Centipawns (100 cp = 1 Pawn). A sigmoid formula maps centipawns to win percentage: P(Win) = 1 / (1 + 10^(-cp / 400)). +100 cp gives ~64% win probability.

2. "Bad Moves Allowed" Mode

Click the top red "Bad Moves Allowed" button to test wrong or illegal moves. You can move any piece anywhere to deliberately test blunders—the Chess Tutor will instantly explain why the move loses material or exposes the king!

3. Move-by-Move Chess Tutor

Every turn, the tutor analyzes the tactical motif (e.g. Center Stake, Knight Outpost, Castling, Blunder) and generates Python code explaining the exact positional calculation.

4. Match Modes & Shortcuts

Switch between Player vs Player, vs Python AI, and AI Simulation. Press Z anytime to Undo.
